# VISION — QuarkSpace

## North star

Owner excerpt, 2026-10-31: “它的主页是我个人网站”。

Owner-confirmed direction, 2026-10-31 (summary): QuarkSpace is a personal website and reusable internet portal — a public identity and portfolio, a home or link for publicly accessible projects, and an access hub for private personal services.

Success means someone can understand the owner's work from the public site, try the projects intended for public use, and the owner can reach authorized personal services across devices through stable entry points. These are goals, not claims that every entry is implemented or verified.

## Boundaries

- The model observatory and Persona Lab are individual product surfaces within this broader portal; neither defines the entire site's purpose.
- Each product or service retains its own source, data, permissions and runtime authority. Hosting or linking does not transfer ownership into this repository.
- Public pages contain only public-safe projections. Private tasks, terminals, credentials and internal APIs keep their existing access boundaries.
- Specific domains, hosting technology and navigation layouts are implementation choices, not permanent product requirements. This direction authorizes no service migration or exposure.
